# Archived source history

This branch preserves source snapshots recovered from the original local
MinuteFlow delivery packages. The snapshots are arranged chronologically so
their differences remain inspectable, but they are reconstructed archival
commits rather than the original development commits.

Available source tags: `v0.1`, `v0.4`, `v0.5`, `v0.5.1`, `v0.5.2`, `v0.6.0`,
`v0.7.0`, and `v0.8.0`.

No independently verifiable source snapshots were found for v0.2, v0.3,
v0.9.0, or v0.10.0. The v0.9.0 and v0.10.0 installation packages are kept in
their GitHub Releases and are documented on the `binary-release-archive`
branch.
